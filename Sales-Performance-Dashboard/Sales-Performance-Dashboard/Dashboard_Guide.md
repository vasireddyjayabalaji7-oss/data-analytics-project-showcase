# Sales Performance Dashboard

## Objective

Analyse sales performance across different regions and products to generate business insights.

## Tools Used

- Microsoft Excel
- Pivot Tables
- Pivot Charts
- Slicers

## KPIs

- Total Sales
- Total Profit
- Total Orders

## Visualisations

### Sales by Region
Create a bar chart showing sales across:
- North
- South
- East
- West

### Profit by Product
Create a column chart showing profits for:
- Laptop
- Monitor
- Printer
- Keyboard
- Mouse

### Monthly Sales Trend
Create a line chart using Date and Sales.

## Dashboard Layout

Top Section:
- Total Sales
- Total Profit
- Total Orders

Middle Section:
- Region-wise Sales Chart
- Product-wise Profit Chart

Bottom Section:
- Monthly Sales Trend

## Expected Outcome

Identify:
- Best-performing region
- Most profitable product
- Overall business performance
